Account

Usernames and passwords are managed in Firebase Authentication, not inside this app. To add or change a login, go to the Firebase console → Authentication → Users.

Manage Admins

Anyone signed in can view everything and log new player transactions. Only emails listed here can mark things paid, edit/delete records, manage players/wallets/super-agent entries, close months, or change settings.

Be careful not to remove your own email, or you'll lose admin access until someone else re-adds you (or you edit it directly in the Firebase console under meta/adminEmailsCSV).

Danger Zone

This permanently deletes every player, transaction and archived month from the database.
